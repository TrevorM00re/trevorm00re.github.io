## Mission Overview
NASA’s Dragonfly mission is a lunar exploration mission to Saturn’s largest moon, Titan. Titan is unique as far as moons go as it has an atmosphere 1.5x thicker than Earth but only 1/7 the gravitational pull. It is believed that the methane and nitrogen rich atmosphere resembles that of Earth during its formation making it a prime location to study how Earth’s early years may have shaped the development of life.

![Titan Moon](/images/TITAN_MOON_IMAGE.jpg)
[Titan Moon](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titan_%28moon%29>)

The mission launches in 2028 with a 6 year long transfer to Saturn. Upon arrival the car sized octocopter will descend into the atmosphere. Once low enough the heat shield will pop off and the octocopter will be lowered into the atmosphere just a few feet to spin up its blades. Once at full speed the octocopter will detach and land itself beginning its 18 month long journey on Titan.

## My Contributions

My work focused on the development of the outer insulation layer and the space HVAC system.

### Insulation

Due to Titan’s cold and thick atmosphere a considerable insulation layer was required to keep the internal temperature at room temperature. I determined issues and designed, ran, and evaluated several TVAC and thermal tests and simulations.

Designed, built, and ran several thermal conductivity tests on coupons of Solimide 16/32, Aerogel Blankets, Basotect-C, and Rohacell to select materials and thicknesses of insulation panels.
Designed, built, and tested thermal expansion tests using a calibrated camera to track feature displacements on coupons through thermal cycles.
Designed, built, and ran several thermal leak tests to determine the effects of a break in the insulation layer.
Designed, built, and ran several tests to determine methods to reduce free convection in the gaps between layers created by velcro attachments, the CFL, harnessing, and external devices/mounting.
Ran Thermal Desktop models to determine the minimum node density required for accurate modeling of various insulation geometries (+/-5% error).
Wrote a MATLAB script to automatically parse thermocouple csv files, perform QA checks, and output standardized tables and graphs.
Prepared materials for a Preliminary Design Review of the insulation design.

### Cold Duct

Designed and built several components for the Thermal Development Test Module, a to scale thermal model of the Dragonfly lander including 80/20 framing, honeycomb paneling, geometrically representative
Designed a venturi for duct air flow measurement by detecting 3-10 Pa drops with +/- 0.5 Pa accuracy. Optimized for minimal flow impedance using online literature and MATLAB.
Designed a large baffle for the 15’ x 15’ x 15’ Titan Chamber in order to reduce inlet nitrogen speed for a no wind speed testing case to verify the performance of the cold duct.
Supported testing to characterize the properties of the DTM cold duct for correlation to Ansys CFD.
